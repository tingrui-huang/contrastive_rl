# PointMaze expert-positive nominal action policy

## Decision

The expert-only K=5 censored mixture is ready for initial ETT integration on
the current PointMaze task. Use
`f4_p30_expert_only_mdn_k5_s0/best.pkl` as the initial checkpoint. Seed 1
reproduces the result and has slightly better validation NLL, while seed 0 has
better overall action-boundary calibration and a marginally better energy
score. Checkpoints remain local and are excluded from Git; configuration,
metrics, the population manifest, evaluation JSON, and plots are versioned.

The remaining coverage issue is specific: the held-out safe-route region has
only 187 transitions from 25 episodes, and both K=5 seeds miss locally
supported `northeast` and `wait` action patterns at the one representative
safe-route context. Main-route and swamp contexts do not show a missing
supported pattern under the same diagnostic. Treat safe-route counterfactuals
with caution until that source population is enlarged or the fit is improved.

## Expert-positive population

The immutable source dataset is `swamp_windy_f4_merged_s0.npz`, SHA-256
`83b4e81d9fca2d66b648c9c34ccdb68196e1acf89e2ca6829e4cb198d27c322c`,
for `point_two_route_swamp_windy_f4_v0` with per-cell swamp probability 0.30.
It contains 6,600 episodes. Each episode has 51 observation rows and 50 real
state-action transitions; the terminal dummy action row is excluded.

Collector and merge provenance establish the population before any outcomes
are inspected:

- Original episodes `[1200, 6000)` are the 4,800 main-source episodes whose
  actions were generated by `scripts.collect_swamp_windy.make_windy_teacher`.
  They provide 240,000 expert-positive transitions and define the nominal
  expert action distribution.
- Original episodes `[0, 1200)` are 1,200 uniform-coverage episodes with
  60,000 transitions. They are excluded from nominal imitation.
- Original episodes `[6000, 6600)` are 600 separately collected blind
  demonstrator episodes with 30,000 transitions. They remain in the full
  dataset for their causal-support role and are excluded from nominal
  imitation.

`teacher_mode` is used only to verify that the collector and merge ranges
match their recorded provenance. Selection does not use success, rewards,
future states, hidden swamp realizations, or other outcomes. Source identifiers
and hidden fields never enter a policy batch. The exact hashes, ranges, mode
counts, and selection predicate are saved in each
`expert_population_manifest.json`.

The split reproduces the historical seed-0, 10% episode split over the full
6,600-episode source and then restricts it to expert-positive episodes. This
leaves 4,321 training episodes with 216,050 transitions and 479 validation
episodes with 23,950 transitions. Original episode identifiers are preserved,
the two sides have no episode overlap, and the byte-identical complete-
trajectory audit found no duplicate trajectory crossing the split.
Normalization is fitted only to selected training trajectories.

## Inputs and action model

The policy models

`b_phi(a | s, g_cmd)`

from learner-visible pre-action inputs only. `s` contains four newest-first XY
frames, or 8 coordinates. `g_cmd` contains 8 commanded-goal coordinates. The
current dataset has one constant goal; keeping the goal follows the project's
conditioning interface but does not demonstrate generalization to unseen
goals. Hidden `U`/swamp bits, source labels, teacher modes, route labels,
rewards, future states, and hindsight goals are excluded.

Actions have two continuous coordinates in `[-1, 1]`. The K=5 baseline is a
conditional mixture of five diagonal Gaussians with two 128-unit SiLU hidden
layers. K=1 uses the same architecture with one component. Sampling draws a
latent Gaussian-mixture action and clips it componentwise to the environment
bounds. Training minimizes the matching censored negative log likelihood:
interior values use Gaussian density and exact clipped boundaries use Gaussian
tail probability. Reported NLL is in nats per two-dimensional action vector
under Lebesgue measure inside `(-1, 1)^2` plus point masses at the clipped
boundaries.

## Training and validation results

All recorded training and validation losses are finite.

- Expert K=5 seed 0: best step 18,500, validation NLL `-0.783374`.
- Expert K=5 seed 1: best step 20,000, validation NLL `-0.790126`.
- Expert K=1 seed 0: best step 19,000, validation NLL `-0.604856`.

All evaluations use the same 479 held-out expert episodes, observed actions,
contexts, shuffled contexts, Gaussian noise, mixture uniforms, and
episode-level resampling protocol. Correct-context versus shuffled-context NLL
is `-0.783374` versus `4.155635` for K=5 seed 0 and `-0.790126` versus
`4.777066` for K=5 seed 1. The corresponding conditional energy scores are
`0.152030` and `0.152124`; K=1 is `0.154528`.

The episode-paired bootstrap gives a K=5 seed-0 minus K=1 NLL difference of
`-0.178518` nats/action vector with 95% interval
`[-0.199804, -0.159442]`. Seed 1 gives `-0.185270` with interval
`[-0.205463, -0.166828]`. The seed-0 minus seed-1 energy-score interval
`[-0.001735, 0.000989]` includes zero.

Observed expert lower-bound frequencies for action coordinates 0 and 1 are
`[0.002129, 0.000877]`; upper-bound frequencies are
`[0.098246, 0.000877]`. K=5 seed 0 predicts analytic lower/upper frequencies
`[0.002445, 0.000506]` and `[0.093581, 0.000828]`. Its sampled frequencies
agree with analytic probabilities within `0.000274`. Seed 1 predicts
`[0.002002, 0.000413]` and `[0.106030, 0.000847]`, with analytic-to-sampled
error below `0.000285`. Final sampled actions have zero out-of-bound values.
The existing episode-separated boundary-only real/generated probes have test
AUC `0.5072` and `0.5095`, respectively, near chance.

The learner-visible region counts are:

- start: 493 transitions from 479 episodes;
- before swamp: 1,233 transitions from 479 episodes;
- moving inside swamp: 1,922 transitions from 454 episodes;
- stationary inside swamp: 1,737 transitions from 43 episodes;
- safe route: 187 transitions from 25 episodes;
- after swamp: 18,338 transitions from 436 episodes.

For K=5 seed 0, regional validation NLL ranges from `-0.9042` after the swamp
to `0.0034` on the sparse safe route. Seed 1 ranges from `-0.9064` after the
swamp to `0.2048` on the safe route. The plotted conditional samples remain
diverse in the well-covered contexts, and all supported local action patterns
are represented there. The safe-route exception described above occurs for
both seeds and for K=1, so unused mixture components are not used as the mode-
collapse criterion.

The historical mixed K=5 and K=1 models are evaluated only on this same
expert-positive validation view. All 479 evaluation episodes belong to those
models' recorded validation sets and none belongs to their training sets.
Their expert-view NLL values are `-0.737451` and `-0.555488`; these are not
compared to their old full-mixture validation numbers. The expert-only models
improve expert-view NLL and energy score relative to the same-size historical
models.

These are checkpoint-selection validation results because the repository has
no untouched nominal-policy test split. A natural-environment rollout was not
used as a selection criterion. The policy lacks demonstrator-only hidden
information, so rollout return would be supplementary rather than evidence of
conditional distribution fit.

## Reproduce

Train K=5 seed 0 and seed 1:

```bash
python -m propensity.train_nominal_policy \
  --dataset datasets/swamp_windy_f4_merged_s0.npz \
  --out-dir artifacts/nominal_policy/f4_p30_expert_only_mdn_k5_s0 \
  --population expert_positive --num-components 5 \
  --hidden-sizes 128,128 --steps 20000 --batch-size 512 \
  --learning-rate 0.0003 --eval-every 500 --seed 0 --split-seed 0

python -m propensity.train_nominal_policy \
  --dataset datasets/swamp_windy_f4_merged_s0.npz \
  --out-dir artifacts/nominal_policy/f4_p30_expert_only_mdn_k5_s1 \
  --population expert_positive --num-components 5 \
  --hidden-sizes 128,128 --steps 20000 --batch-size 512 \
  --learning-rate 0.0003 --eval-every 500 --seed 1 --split-seed 0
```

Train the K=1 comparison:

```bash
python -m propensity.train_nominal_policy \
  --dataset datasets/swamp_windy_f4_merged_s0.npz \
  --out-dir artifacts/nominal_policy/f4_p30_expert_only_unimodal_k1_s0 \
  --population expert_positive --num-components 1 \
  --hidden-sizes 128,128 --steps 20000 --batch-size 512 \
  --learning-rate 0.0003 --eval-every 500 --seed 0 --split-seed 0
```

Run the API smoke test:

```bash
python -m scripts.smoke_nominal_policy
```

Run the common expert-validation evaluation:

```bash
python -m propensity.eval_expert_nominal_policy \
  --dataset datasets/swamp_windy_f4_merged_s0.npz \
  --model expert_k5_s0=artifacts/nominal_policy/f4_p30_expert_only_mdn_k5_s0 \
  --model expert_k5_s1=artifacts/nominal_policy/f4_p30_expert_only_mdn_k5_s1 \
  --model expert_k1_s0=artifacts/nominal_policy/f4_p30_expert_only_unimodal_k1_s0 \
  --model mixed_k5_s0=artifacts/nominal_policy/f4_p30_mdn_k5_s0 \
  --model mixed_k1_s0=artifacts/nominal_policy/f4_p30_unimodal_k1_s0 \
  --compare expert_k5_s0:expert_k5_s1 \
  --compare expert_k5_s0:expert_k1_s0 \
  --compare expert_k5_s1:expert_k1_s0 \
  --compare expert_k5_s0:mixed_k5_s0 \
  --compare expert_k1_s0:mixed_k1_s0 \
  --out-dir artifacts/nominal_policy/f4_p30_expert_only_evaluation_s01 \
  --boundary-samples 64 --energy-contexts 1024 --energy-samples 32 \
  --representative-samples 1024 --local-neighbors 256 \
  --bootstrap-replicates 2000 \
  --plot-labels expert_k5_s0,expert_k5_s1,expert_k1_s0 --seed 917
```

Sample one or several observational reference actions:

```python
import jax
from propensity.nominal_policy import load_nominal_policy

policy = load_nominal_policy(
    'artifacts/nominal_policy/f4_p30_expert_only_mdn_k5_s0')
x_prime = policy.sample(observation, jax.random.PRNGKey(7))
draws = policy.sample(observation, jax.random.PRNGKey(8), num_samples=32)

# The equivalent separated-input form preserves arbitrary leading shapes.
draws = policy.sample(
    state, jax.random.PRNGKey(9), num_samples=32, goal=commanded_goal)
```

The verified interface returns shapes `[2]` and `[K, 2]` for an unbatched
observation, is exactly reproducible for the same key, returns finite values,
and enforces action bounds. Future ETT observational references and diagonal
fitting should use this expert-positive population. The excluded coverage and
blind-demonstrator data remain unchanged for their separately defined roles
and must not redefine the nominal expert distribution.
