## Replay and goals

Only obs and act are read from the original 6600-episode mixed-collector dataset. Use the established full-source partition: exclude the first 660 episodes in seed-0 permutation, leaving 5940 training episodes across all behavior sources. Original actor/critic upstream training used all episodes; the continuation partition does not undo that reuse. No prior native-continuation arrays, hidden fields, masks, rewards or scorer predictions enter training.

Run 2000 individual gradient updates per arm and learner seed. At updates 0,100,...,1900, B/C generate 256 trajectories of exactly three transitions from uniform training-episode contexts with uniform original time 0..47 inclusive. Thus every segment ends by the original 50-step boundary. Their complete F4 stacks come from recorded observations. Commanded rollout goal stays tiled (8.5,3.5). Each step independently draws nominal x_prime and an execution action from the current stochastic actor, then samples the unchanged ETT. The current actor refreshes every 100 updates. The replay retains only the latest 256 segments (768 transitions).

Use original TrajectoryBuffer geometric future sampling: uniform trajectory then uniform anchor i, and j>i with probability proportional to .95^(j-i) over available future observations. Generated segments have FOUR observations and THREE actions, never padded into 51 observations. Positive hindsight goal is the FULL eight-coordinate achieved F4 state at j of that SAME segment. Commanded goal is never automatically a positive. This is a truncated three-step MC future distribution, unlike the longer offline window; it is an explicit approximation and potential distribution shift, matched in B/C.

Each batch has 256 anchors. Synthetic counts follow floor((u+1)*256/10)-floor(u*256/10), giving 25/26 and exactly 10% every five updates (51200/512000 per model arm). Draw a common full offline batch, replace its last n rows, then apply a matched random permutation. All 256 rows/columns participate in the original NCE loss. Synthetic rows are positive anchors and synthetic goals are also ordinary in-batch negatives for other rows, including cross-source negatives. No per-source loss reweighting; B/C have identical counts. Actor random-goal rolling and BC act on the mixed batch using the existing objective. Generated actions are current-policy self-imitation targets for the BC half, which can influence behavior.

The MC objective reads only observation/action. Its unused reward, discount and next_action slots carry NaN sentinels to detect accidental use, not invented rewards, terminal/death labels or an action beyond the rollout boundary. next_observation contains the actual generated successor and same relabeled goal. There is no TD path, continuation bootstrap or synthetic termination. Actual fixed-goal model rewards are diagnostic only. x_prime stays in separate auxiliary records and never enters the learner's 16-dimensional observation or two-dimensional action.

## Interpretation and stopping

Frozen diagonal preservation and global execution-action Lipschitzness do not establish absorption, reachability or multistep correctness. Do not fix stationarity by declaring death or modify the ETT. A native C-over-B gain alone is insufficient because B is action-invariant. Assess C-A as the stronger comparison. Model-only gains suggest possible exploitation, not successful native policy improvement. Describe effects of the short future-goal window and mixed BC as integration limitations, distinct from bugs. Answer the five user questions and stop after the small experiment. Do not fit readouts, alter ETT objectives, train alpha=.5, alternate models/actors, launch further families or push automatically.
